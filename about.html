What is EverSong

EverSong is a small, free browser toy that composes an endless, ever-changing piece of ambient music using the Web Audio API — no samples, no accounts, nothing to install. Pick a mood, press play, and let it drift. It works fully offline once loaded, and runs entirely on your device: no audio or musical data is generated on or sent to a server.
